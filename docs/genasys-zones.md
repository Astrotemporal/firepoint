# Genasys / LA County zone source check (2026-09-26)

**Implemented now:** an ordinary external [Genasys Protect map](https://protect.genasys.com/en?lat=34.2147&lng=-118.2629&z=11.3) link on `/prepare` and the offline fallback's “When you reconnect” list. The URL's `lat`, `lng` and `z` **only position the camera over Glendale**. They do not look up an address or attest to a zone or order. Search your address on the external site and follow the responsible agency. Firepoint does not fetch, embed, cache, redraw or claim an active Genasys zone; it has no resident lookup and no all-clear. After PR #23 added Spanish and Eastern Armenian guide text, the new Genasys link and its caution appear **only in the English guide**. The translated source lists keep Glendale Fire’s existing Know Your Zone link; adding Genasys there needs fluent review of both label and caveat. The static offline fallback is English-only.

## Separate publisher sources for a possible future overlay

- LA County EOC's [standing zone FeatureServer](https://services.arcgis.com/RmCCgQtiZLDCtblq/arcgis/rest/services/Genasys_Evacuation_Zone_Live_Feed/FeatureServer/0) ([item `036cd517980741b0ab4a29d484d0b54f`](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=036cd517980741b0ab4a29d484d0b54f)) held **55 published `GLN` features** when checked. They include county zone IDs and publisher URLs, but a `Normal` standing zone is **not** today's order or warning. The layer's dataset edit date was 2025-05-30; checked Glendale boundary records were last updated in January 2025. Record both the dataset and per-zone revision. The supplied map center intersected `US-CA-XLA-GLN-E008` in that dated data; **this is not a resident address lookup**.
- The County's [active-alert view](https://services.arcgis.com/RmCCgQtiZLDCtblq/arcgis/rest/services/Public_Emergency_Map_GENASYS_Alert_Warning_Area/FeatureServer/0) ([item `e8285c6509ec4269b5cd2aef02f41c8f`](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=e8285c6509ec4269b5cd2aef02f41c8f)) showed **zero records at the check time**; it is a separate view and may include warning, order, advisory or shelter-in-place statuses. Zero records **never** proves no emergency, coverage or all-clear. Its metadata and filtering text do not fully agree, so verify exact statuses before using them.
- [Cal OES's statewide active view](https://services.arcgis.com/BLN4oKB0N1YSgvY8/arcgis/rest/services/CA_EVACUATIONS_CalOESHosted_view/FeatureServer/0) is another separately checked aggregate. A rebuild may briefly return empty, and older active-looking records can survive. Never interpret an empty response or an undated status as safe.

Genasys is the **vendor**, LA County EOC operates these published GIS layers, and the actual Glendale evacuation-order **issuer** is not established by the layer metadata. Glendale Fire's own [Know Your Zone](https://www.glendaleca.gov/government/departments/fire-department/other-links/emergency-preparedness-response/know-your-zone) page needs a human browser check; automated requests were blocked. Genasys' [terms](https://genasys.com/genasys-protect-terms-of-use/) restrict automated extraction and redistribution; **do not scrape its SPA, tiles or internal APIs, iframe it, reuse its branding, or use a public WFS item containing someone else's key**. LA County's ArcGIS data has separate publication terms and required attribution; confirm public reuse, issuer authority, cadence and attribution with the County/Glendale before publishing a derived overlay.

If those checks pass, build a **server-only, status-carrying zone adapter**: versioned polygons/zone IDs and source checks for dated standing boundaries; separately published active notices with issuer, original URL/text/status and per-record times. Fail closed on partial/paginated results, schema drift, stale or missing issuer timestamps, layer outages and mismatched zone IDs. Cache within permitted cadence and retain last-good **with its last-good time**, never as “current.” Use a new polygon layer; the current map's circle-based `Hazard` type is not an evacuation zone. Distinguish official order/warning polygons from standing zones, dated fire perimeters, private marks and model-only planning bands. Test these cases with clearly synthetic fixtures before connecting any public UI.
