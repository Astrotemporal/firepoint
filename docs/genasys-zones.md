# Genasys / LA County zone source check (2026-09-26)

**Implemented now:** an ordinary external [Genasys Protect map](https://protect.genasys.com/en?lat=34.2147&lng=-118.2629&z=11.3) link on `/prepare` and the offline fallback's “When you reconnect” list. The URL's `lat`, `lng` and `z` **only position the camera over Glendale**. They do not look up an address or attest to a zone or order. Search your address on the external site and follow the responsible agency. Firepoint does not fetch, embed, cache, redraw or claim an active Genasys zone; it has no resident lookup and no all-clear. After PR #23 added Spanish and Eastern Armenian guide text, the new Genasys link and its caution appear **only in the English guide**. The translated source lists keep Glendale Fire’s existing Know Your Zone link; adding Genasys there needs fluent review of both label and caveat. The static offline fallback is English-only.

## Separate publisher sources for a possible future overlay

- LA County EOC's [standing zone FeatureServer](https://services.arcgis.com/RmCCgQtiZLDCtblq/arcgis/rest/services/Genasys_Evacuation_Zone_Live_Feed/FeatureServer/0) ([item `036cd517980741b0ab4a29d484d0b54f`](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=036cd517980741b0ab4a29d484d0b54f)) held **55 published `GLN` features** when checked. They include county zone IDs and publisher URLs, but a `Normal` standing zone is **not** today's order or warning. **Do not read status from this boundary snapshot:** across the layer, 19 non-`Normal` rows still say `Evacuation Order`, `Evacuation Warning` or `Advisory` for old Palisades/other events. The layer's data-last-edit is 2025-05-30 despite a "checked every 10 minutes" snippet; checked Glendale boundary records were last updated in January 2025. A boundaries-only adapter must drop `AlertReason`/incident fields entirely and label the vintage. Record both the dataset and per-zone revision. The supplied map center intersected `US-CA-XLA-GLN-E008` in that dated data; **this is not a resident address lookup**.
- The County's [active-alert view](https://services.arcgis.com/RmCCgQtiZLDCtblq/arcgis/rest/services/Public_Emergency_Map_GENASYS_Alert_Warning_Area/FeatureServer/0) ([item `e8285c6509ec4269b5cd2aef02f41c8f`](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=e8285c6509ec4269b5cd2aef02f41c8f)) showed **zero records at the check time**; it is a separate view and may include warning, order, advisory or shelter-in-place statuses. Zero records **never** proves no emergency, coverage or all-clear. Its view filter includes `Advisory` and `Shelter in Place` as well as warning/order, contrary to the snippet. The public view's service `/sources` points at a **private** `GENASYS_Alert_Warning_Area_NEW` source, not the older standing snapshot; its live cadence cannot be verified from an empty view. The rows lack a reliable issuing-agency field. **No active-status overlay** until LA County confirms cadence/issuer semantics and a positive record can be validated; always show publisher last-edit and Firepoint last-check separately.
- [Cal OES's statewide active view](https://services.arcgis.com/BLN4oKB0N1YSgvY8/arcgis/rest/services/CA_EVACUATIONS_CalOESHosted_view/FeatureServer/0) is another separately checked aggregate. Its license restricts use to Cal OES personnel and supporting partners, and stale active-looking 2023 records survive. **Do not use it as a resident Firepoint feed.** Never interpret an empty response or an undated status as safe.

Genasys is the **vendor**, LA County EOC operates these published GIS layers, and the actual Glendale evacuation-order **issuer** is not established by the layer metadata. Glendale Fire's own [Know Your Zone](https://www.glendaleca.gov/government/departments/fire-department/other-links/emergency-preparedness-response/know-your-zone) page needs a human browser check; automated requests were blocked. Genasys' [terms](https://genasys.com/genasys-protect-terms-of-use/) restrict automated extraction and redistribution; **do not scrape its SPA, tiles or internal APIs, iframe it, reuse its branding, or use a public WFS item containing someone else's key**. LA County's [eGIS terms](https://egis-lacounty.hub.arcgis.com/pages/terms-of-use) permit copying/adapting County-published data with citation and no endorsement, but the County's right to sublicense **Genasys-derived geometry** is not explicit. Get written confirmation from LA County eGIS/OEM or Genasys before redistributing polygons; a public ArcGIS endpoint alone is not that confirmation.

After written geometry-reuse confirmation, a **server-only boundaries adapter** could export versioned `GLN` polygons/zone IDs with dataset and per-zone vintages, source checks, County attribution and **no `AlertReason` or incident status**. Reject partial/paginated responses, schema drift, coverage gaps or a changed record count; preserve last-good with its time, never call it current. Test synthetic stale-`Order` rows to prove all status fields are stripped. Use a neutral Mapbox polygon layer; the map's circle-based `Hazard` type is not a zone. A **separate later** active-notice adapter would need proven cadence, issuer identity/status mapping, source times, outage handling and rights before any agency red/yellow fill. Private marks and planning bands remain separate. Neither adapter changes routes without independent official closure/agency-route evidence.

## Map and route boundary (future, not in this PR)

With explicit reuse rights and verified issuer/coverage, Mapbox can render **standing zone boundaries in a neutral style** from an allowlisted server adapter. A separate active status may use warning-yellow or order-red **only when the issuing agency publishes that status and its exact applicable polygon**. Source/issue/as-of/retrieval times, geometry revision, stale/failed checks and an agency link must stay visible. Do not turn the map center, a private mark, a crowd density cell, or a scaled circle around a fire into an evacuation zone.

A zone says **who an agency is notifying**, not that a road is blocked or a destination is safe. Routing needs a separately verified road-closure/agency-route source and vetted destinations. The current Mapbox Directions adapter can request alternatives, but the planner discards all except its first picked route. A later route-comparison card may keep two **distinct provider-returned** candidates with their request time and evidence checks, never fabricate a second route when a canyon has one exit, and label unchecked closures as unknown. A native Apple/Google Maps handoff can recalculate a different path; Firepoint cannot place a persistent widget inside those native apps or certify their turn-by-turn route. No route overlay is enabled here.
