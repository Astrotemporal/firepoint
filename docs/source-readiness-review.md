# Draft: Glendale resident source-readiness review

**Research snapshot:** 2026-09-27 UTC. **Scope:** source and publication feasibility only; no resident address or personalized point lookup, app integration, operational validation, publisher permission, or legal approval. Public item/service metadata and broad nonpersonal queries were inspected. An observed empty feed or dated metadata is not a current incident assessment. Recheck all publisher documents before any release.

## Release decision

**No resident-facing “SAFE,” “all clear,” current road-open, matched official evacuation-zone, or “OPEN shelter near you” claim is ready.** A public URL, accessible REST query, ArcGIS feature, or map icon alone does not prove data rights, issuing authority, full geographic coverage, operational status, or current validity. A successful zero-row result means only that the queried source returned no matching published records within its established coverage at that check. A failed, malformed, out-of-coverage, rebuilding, or stale source is **unavailable/partial**, not empty-success. An absent active record is not proof an issuer retracted it.

**Safe interim:** show dated source descriptions and direct official links, with explicit gaps; invite residents to check local fire/law enforcement and sign up for official alerts. Do not copy an agency instruction into an app-issued verdict. LA County's [emergency page](https://lacounty.gov/emergency/) links its [Emergency Map](https://lacounty.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=695f0c805ba046b28c84da79bbf252dc) and says that map is for regional emergencies; check local fire/law enforcement and media for smaller localized incidents. The map item warns boundaries may be approximate, updates may take hours, and zoom can mislead. Official zone-information link (manual lookup, not an app API): [Glendale Fire Know Your Zone](https://www.glendaleca.gov/government/departments/fire-department/other-links/emergency-preparedness-response/know-your-zone), which needs human verification because automated access was blocked. Direct alert registration: [Glendale](https://public.alertsense.com/SignUp/Default.aspx?regionid=1916) and [LA County](https://alertlacounty.genasys.com/portal/en/register), [county guidance](https://ready.lacounty.gov/emergency-notifications/). Neither signup is this app's notice feed.

## Authority and rights are separate gates

1. **Authority:** record the actual issuing fire, police, or emergency agency, jurisdiction, original record/URL, issued/updated time if published, status/retraction semantics, and which aggregator merely republishes it. A Cal OES, LA EOC, FEMA, or Genasys operator is not automatically the issuer of each order. Ask Glendale to confirm that it adopts any third-party zone ID/boundary for the relevant incident and jurisdiction.
2. **Rights:** [LA County eGIS Terms of Use](https://egis-lacounty.hub.arcgis.com/pages/terms-of-use) license copying, publishing, distributing, transmitting, adapting and commercial/personal use of County ArcGIS data, subject to terms. They prohibit implying County endorsement, disclaim accuracy and continuous availability, reserve terms-based termination, and recommend a dataset citation. The [LA EOC warning/order item](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/e8285c6509ec4269b5cd2aef02f41c8f?f=json) explicitly points users of County REST URLs to these terms. [Genasys Protect terms](https://genasys.com/genasys-protect-terms-of-use/) separately restrict automated extraction and redistribution; do not scrape its SPA, tiles or internal API, embed the map, or reuse its branding. County rights to sublicense Genasys-derived geometry need confirmation. The County license does **not** authorize claiming operational completeness or affiliation. Obtain County/issuer confirmation for this use.
3. **Rights conflict:** the [Cal OES catalog item](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/c0dd2a8779764c26910b83a7e974ee66?f=json) says no special restrictions were supplied, but the [actual service item](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/3ba86748155e4582954d6a0c217ecd3c?f=json) associated with its FeatureServer says “Public Use” **and** “for use by Cal OES personnel and supporting partners to conduct emergency management operations”; Cal OES does not certify the multiagency contents. This conflict is **unresolved**. Ask [Cal OES GIS](mailto:gissupport@caloes.ca.gov) for written civilian-app API, caching, display, and redistribution terms, and check each originating county/Genasys license. Do not treat a publicly readable URL or a generic item label as a sublicense.
4. **Other publishers:** inspect item-level licenses and underlying third-party terms separately. “No licenseInfo provided” does not grant redistribution. Linking to an official page is distinct from harvesting, storing, republishing or embedding its content. Legal/publisher review is required before deployment.

## Required publisher confirmations and engineering gates

- **Glendale Fire/Police/Emergency Management:** current issuing agency and official alert URL for Glendale; whether it recognizes County/Genasys zone names, versions, boundaries and current order issuance; incident-specific versus standing zones; permission to identify city as issuer. **Glendale Public Works/Police:** city-street closure canonical feed, update time, retractions, conditions and rights.
- **LA County EOC/eGIS/Genasys:** geographic and incident coverage (especially smaller events), source vs original issuer, field enums, official record links, alert start/end/retraction, geometry accuracy, lookup/boundary version, delivery cadence/lag, outage and empty-view behavior, Genasys-derived publication rights and proper attribution. **LA DPW:** supported machine-readable closure export, rights and city/unincorporated boundary.
- **Cal OES GIS:** reconcile actual service-item restrictive wording with catalog metadata; county-by-county completeness, licensing of county/Genasys sources, feed rebuilding and stale records, field/status/issue/withdraw semantics, allowed polling/cache/display. **FEMA:** OpenShelters reuse rights, verified source timestamp, Red Cross synchronization and operational status/closure lifecycle, coverage, permissible cache and attribution. **211/Red Cross:** official API and reuse terms if proposed. **Caltrans:** rights and status semantics for LCS and any separately sourced CHP/CHIN feed.
- **Adapter tests before any conditional feed is released:** allowlisted official endpoint; schema validation; bounds and coverage; issuer/aggregator/original URL; stable ID; distinction among *issued*, *source updated*, *fetched successfully*, *last attempted* and *stale after*; time-zone rendering; planned vs active vs ended; duplicate/conflicting notices; schema changes, pagination and size caps, HTTP errors/429, transient zero during rebuild, offline last-good labeled **last synced**, and explicit retraction. A zero-source registry is unavailable, not an empty success. Do not silently combine conflicting agencies into one verdict or substitute feed edit time for an issuer's time. Do not send a resident's precise location to third parties or put it in browser URLs/logs; no location request was part of this research.

This review is a **draft integration gate**, not an agency-endorsed safety product or a legal opinion. No observed count, sampled incident record, or sample location from research is app data.
