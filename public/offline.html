SET

Fire nearby or a Red Flag Warning

Get your household and home ready to leave. Keep checking official sources.

  1. Watch for official alerts
    Check Glendale Alerts, Know Your Zone and local news. Keep your phone charged.
  2. Load the car and park facing out
    Put go bags in, keep the tank full, and keep your keys with you.
  3. Keep pets close
    Bring them inside and have carriers and leashes ready.
  4. Close windows and doors, but leave them unlocked
    Close vents and garage doors too. Firefighters may need to get in.
  5. Move burnable things away from the house
    Bring in patio furniture, doormats and trash bins. Take down flammable curtains.
  6. Leave hoses connected and lights on
    Firefighters can use the hoses. Lights help them see your home in smoke.
  7. Wear protective clothing
    Long sleeves and pants in cotton or wool, sturdy shoes, and an N95 mask for smoke.
GO

Leaving

Leave early. You do not have to wait for an order if you feel unsafe.

  1. Follow official instructions first
    If authorities tell you to leave, go right away, using the routes they give.
  2. Take your go bag, people and pets
    Do not go back for belongings.
  3. Drive with headlights on and windows closed
    Set air to recirculate. Watch for fire crews and people on foot.
  4. Tell someone where you are going
    Texts often get through when calls do not.
  5. If you are trapped, call 911
    Give your location. Help may be delayed, so turn on lights to help rescuers find you.
  6. Do not return until officials say it is safe
    Hot ash, embers and damaged lines can remain after the fire passes.

Guidance adapted from CAL FIRE Ready, Set, Go! and Ready.gov. Always follow instructions from local officials.
